## 2. SDXL is Technically Impressive — But Its Real Bottleneck Is the Data

### Overview

Stable Diffusion XL (SDXL) represents a major advancement in latent diffusion models, yet its ultimate performance depends far more on the quality of training data than on architectural complexity.

This article explains why scaling datasets, improving caption quality, reducing noise, and better data curation remain the largest opportunities for future image generation models.

### Topics Covered

- Diffusion Models
- Stable Diffusion XL Architecture
- Latent Diffusion
- Two-Stage Generation Pipeline
- Cross-Attention
- Text Conditioning
- Dataset Quality
- Caption Engineering
- Synthetic Data
- Data Filtering
- Future of Diffusion Models

### Key Takeaways

- Model architecture is no longer the biggest bottleneck
- Data quality drives image quality
- Better captions improve semantic understanding
- Future research should prioritise data pipelines
